01Master's thesis, solo

AR Navigation for Museum Environments

For my master's thesis I built an indoor navigation app for museums that combines a classic map with AR directions shown right in the room. Tracking uses Vuforia Area Targets made from a Matterport scan of the building. I compared several navigation techniques in a study that ran in the museum during regular opening hours.

Unity (C#), Vuforia, Matterport, Android

02Exhibition, team

Youtopia

An exhibition about urban utopias. I was on the build side: I set up the optical scanners (a Raspberry Pi with up to three cameras each) that read every visitor's companion object, installed the VR station and kept the exhibition running until it closed.

  • ADC Talent Award 2024Exhibition, Bronze
  • CommAwards 2023Talent Exhibition, Silver

Unity (C#), Python, Raspberry Pi
